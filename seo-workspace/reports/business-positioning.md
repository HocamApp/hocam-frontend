# Business, Audience, Positioning, and Conversion Brief

## Verified product facts

- Hocam is a marketplace, not a recorded-course library or conventional tutoring center.
- Public supply is limited to verified, publicly approved tutors.
- The core audience is students preparing for YKS, including TYT and AYT needs.
- Tutors are high-ranking university students or graduates whose public profiles expose relevant education and YKS information.
- Lessons are online and one-to-one.
- Students can compare tutors, communicate, and book within the product.
- The product uses package credits, but no live payment provider is connected. Package or ledger data is not proof of settled payment.

## Primary audiences

1. YKS students who need help in one or more subjects
2. Parents evaluating tutor quality, safety, fit, and value
3. High-ranking university students seeking to teach after verification

## Core customer problems

- “I do not know which tutor is right for my subject, level, schedule, and budget.”
- “I want evidence that a tutor understands the YKS experience.”
- “I want to compare real profiles and reviews before committing.”
- “I need one-to-one help, not another broad video library or group course.”

## Positioning

Recommended concise positioning:

> Hocam helps YKS students choose and study one-to-one with verified, high-ranking tutors whose profiles, subjects, availability, and student feedback can be compared directly.

This is an internal positioning statement, not approved website copy.

## Defensible differentiation

- Tutor-level choice instead of one generic program
- Verification before public listing
- YKS rank as a transparent, relevant signal
- Subject, schedule, price, review, and teaching-style comparison
- Integrated communication, booking, and online lesson workflow

## What Hocam should not claim

- Guaranteed score or rank improvement
- “Best” or “number one” without defensible independent evidence
- Settled payment, refund, or tutor earnings before a payment provider exists
- Pedagogical or professional teaching credentials not actually held
- Therapy, clinical, or regulated educational guidance
- Nationwide physical coverage unless in-person service is introduced and verified

## Conversion model

Primary conversion:

- A qualified student starts and completes the approved tutor-contact or booking flow.

Supporting conversions:

- Tutor profile view
- Tutor comparison/filter interaction
- Trial request
- Return visit to a saved/favorite tutor
- Tutor verification completion

## Bad-fit traffic

- Users seeking recorded full-course packages only
- Users seeking face-to-face local lessons, unless that service is introduced
- Non-YKS subjects or exams not supported by current strategy
- Queries seeking answer keys, pirated books, or copyrighted exam PDFs
- “Free” intent when there is no matching free product

## Open founder decisions

1. Whether the public acquisition scope is YKS-only or may include DGS/KPSS already present in the subject database
2. Whether parents are direct account users or only decision influencers
3. Whether pricing factors may be explained publicly
4. Whether a public marketing homepage can replace the root login screen
5. Whether tutor verification methodology may be described publicly in detail
6. Whether model-training crawlers should be allowed independently of AI search crawlers
