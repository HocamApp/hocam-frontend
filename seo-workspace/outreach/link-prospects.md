# Link Prospecting and Digital PR Plan

Status: research only. No outreach was sent and no contact details were invented.

## Linkable-asset constraint

Hocam currently has a public tutor directory, but no strong editorial or data asset that gives independent publishers a compelling reason to link. Outreach should not begin until one of these exists:

1. A transparent report on verified YKS tutor prices by subject, with methodology and sample size
2. An anonymized study of what students consider when choosing a YKS tutor
3. A practical tutor-verification and safety checklist for parents
4. A maintained, neutral guide to choosing between tutoring, coaching, and online-course products
5. A free worksheet or comparison tool that does not collect unnecessary personal data

The strongest initial angle is an anonymized, regularly updated YKS private-lesson price and tutor-choice report based on real Hocam supply.

## Prospect categories to develop

1. Independent YKS guidance publishers
2. Parent education publications
3. University student and alumni publications
4. Education-technology media
5. Scholarship and student-support organizations
6. Ethical directories with editorial review
7. Podcasts/newsletters serving YKS students and parents

Avoid:

- Paid-link lists
- Unmoderated bulk directories
- Scraped “guest post” networks
- Irrelevant university pages
- Mass email campaigns
- Fake scholarship or research claims

## Qualification score

Score each prospect from 0–2 on:

- Topical relevance
- Editorial independence
- Audience overlap
- Page-level fit
- Ability to cite the asset naturally
- Reputation and safety

Do not contact prospects scoring below 8/12.

## Contact-discovery rules

- Use only public author, masthead, contact, or editorial pages.
- Record the exact source URL for every contact.
- Do not guess email patterns.
- Prefer the relevant author or editor over a general inbox.
- Respect community and submission rules.
- Do not use personal student data in a pitch.

## Outreach launch gate

1. Founder approves the asset.
2. Privacy review confirms aggregation thresholds.
3. Every claim and sample size is reproducible.
4. A named spokesperson is approved.
5. Ten high-fit prospects are individually reviewed.
6. Messages are personalized and rate-limited.
7. Replies and opt-outs are logged.
