# Competitive Landscape

Research date: 29 July 2026
Evidence: official websites, live search-result samples, public robots files, and public sitemaps
Limitations: no OpenSEO/domain-metric/backlink provider and no first-party Search Console data

## Market read

The named competitors span different business models. Özel Ders Alanı is the closest large marketplace. Kunduz and Doping Hafıza are broader learning ecosystems. Baykuş is primarily mentoring/coaching. Hocam should not imitate their breadth. Its best opening is a narrow, transparent directory for students who want to choose a verified, high-ranking YKS tutor for one-to-one help.

The biggest barrier is not one named competitor. It is the combination of:

- Very large tutor/location directories
- Subject-specialist sites with focused landing pages
- Broad platforms with years of brand demand
- Extensive educational content libraries

The most winnable area is the intersection of:

- YKS subject intent
- One-to-one online lessons
- Verified or high-ranking tutors
- Transparent tutor comparison

## Deep-dive observations

### Baykuş Mentörlük

Biggest lesson:

- A narrow product is easier to explain than a generic marketplace. Its homepage immediately states one-to-one coaching and separates coaching from test-club offers.

Vulnerability:

- It is not built around choosing a subject tutor from comparable profiles.

Do not copy:

- Package-led coaching language if Hocam is selling direct subject instruction.

Hocam response:

- Explain the distinction between subject tutoring, mentoring, and broad exam programs.
- Let the tutor profile and verification evidence carry trust.

### Kunduz

Biggest lesson:

- Breadth is expressed through concrete features: question solving, coaching, live classes, test clubs, question banks, and parent support.

Vulnerability:

- Breadth can obscure the user who simply wants a chosen tutor for a specific weakness.

Do not copy:

- “All-in-one” positioning; Hocam cannot and should not compete on content volume.

Hocam response:

- Own “choose the person who teaches you” rather than “buy an entire ecosystem.”

### Doping Hafıza

Biggest lesson:

- Multiple content formats capture the whole search journey: product, course, FAQ, video, review, calculator, and parent/student questions.

Vulnerability:

- It is a product/program decision more than a tutor-selection marketplace.

Do not copy:

- Content scale without editorial resources, evidence, and maintenance.

Hocam response:

- Publish fewer, stronger decision pages linked directly to real tutor supply.
- Use original anonymized marketplace insights only after privacy review.

### Özel Ders Alanı

Biggest lesson:

- Category, subject, city, district, blog, and tutor-profile templates create enormous surface area.

Vulnerability:

- A huge general directory creates noise and thin-page risk. It cannot be as specific about verified YKS rank and exam fit on every result.

Do not copy:

- Thousands of city/district doorway pages when Hocam is online-first.

Hocam response:

- Build subject-and-exam pages only where real verified tutor supply exists.
- Make verification and YKS relevance explicit.

- Large public tutor directories
- Topic-specific educational guides

## Authority and backlink notes

No backlink index is connected, so referring-domain counts, authority scores, anchor distributions, and link gaps are unknown. Sitemap scale and ranking presence are not substitutes for backlink evidence.

Directional inference:

- Doping Hafıza and Kunduz likely benefit from long-lived brands, product coverage, and external mentions.
- Özel Ders Alanı benefits from a large public URL and profile ecosystem.
- Smaller exact-subject sites can still compete when their intent match is much tighter.

## Strategic gaps

1. “Verified YKS-ranked tutor” directory
2. Subject-specific tutor discovery backed by live supply
3. Clear explanation of how verification works
4. How to choose between private tutoring, coaching, and a digital course
5. Transparent price factors without inventing market averages
6. Parent trust and safety guidance
7. Tutor profile pages that machines and users can read without client-only rendering

## Priority actions

1. Ship the non-visual technical foundation.
2. Connect Search Console before choosing content by volume.
3. Approve a public marketing homepage or a crawlable public landing route.
4. Create the TYT mathematics cluster first only if real tutor supply supports it.
5. Add verification and “how it works” pages.
6. Expand by subject after conversion evidence.
7. Build original linkable research only after enough anonymized marketplace data exists.
